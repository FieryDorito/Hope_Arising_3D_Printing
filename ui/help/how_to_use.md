### Making a print, step by step

The steps are listed on the left. When you finish a step, the next one opens by itself.
You can always click an earlier step to go back and change something.

**1 · Find the part**
- **Describe it:** type what the customer needs in your own words, for example
  *"a cap for the end of a 25 mm pipe"*, and press **Find part**.
- **Or browse:** open **Browse parts**, search or pick a category, and press **Choose**.

**2 · Options and size**
- Measure the customer's item **with a ruler or calipers**, in millimetres.
- Each box says what it measures. Read it carefully: *inside* and *outside* diameter are different.
- If the AI shows **Ask the customer**, ask that question before you go on.
- Pick the **material**: PLA for everyday indoor parts, PETG for water, heat or sun.
- Pick the **strength**: Strong for parts that hold weight (hooks, clamps, hinges).
- Press **Next: check the part**.

**3 · Check the part**
- Turn the 3D picture with your finger or mouse. Check the size numbers.
- Wrong? Press **Change settings**. Right? Press **Next: make print file**.

**4 · Save and print**
- Write the customer's name and phone number (helps you find the order later).
- Put the SD card in the computer and press **Save to card**.
- When it says it is done, take the card out, put it in the printer, load the right
  filament, and choose the file on the printer screen.

### Past prints

Every finished print file is kept under **Past prints** on the left.
- **Save to card again** prints the exact same file again.
- **Change and print again** opens it at step 2 so you can fix the size.
  If a part was too tight or too loose, write that in the notes, then change it by 0.2 – 0.5 mm.
- Change the **status** as the order moves: Waiting to print → Printing → Done → Picked up.

### If something goes wrong
- **"OpenSCAD" or "PrusaSlicer is not installed":** tell the student team.
- **The card is not found:** take it out, put it back in, and press **Look again**.
- **No internet:** describing a part with the AI will not work, but **Browse parts** still does.
- **Long prints:** if a print takes more than 2 hours, make sure the generator is ready
  in case the power goes off.
